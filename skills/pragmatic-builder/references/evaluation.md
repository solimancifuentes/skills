# Evaluation protocol

Use this protocol for calibration, stronger validation, or an explicitly authorized update to the voice. It is not a required step in ordinary drafting. Defined cases and synthetic examples describe intended behavior; only recorded trials establish what happened under their particular conditions.

## Scope and compare

Match the test to the change and decision. A broad capability or voice revision uses the eight scenarios below, comparing ordinary writing without Pragmatic Builder, the current skill, and the candidate. A narrow repair compares the candidate with the relevant baseline on fresh affected cases; add a regression control only where the change could affect another register. Documentation-only changes need no writing trial.

Before a batch, use a small preflight to verify the intended profile and context/tool isolation. Resolve contamination before scaling. Keep briefs, evidence, audience, length, tools, and generator settings equivalent. Record revisions, generator, available settings, source cutoff, and date; disclose consequential differences.

Generate drafts independently in fresh sessions with minimal relevant context and the same source packet or conversation excerpts. Exclude earlier drafts, critiques, evaluation hints, and instructions to beat another condition. Include adequate but incomplete briefs when testing autonomy; a complete intake form can conceal unnecessary questions.

## Scenario set

Use new material beyond the calibration examples. Match length and effort to the assignment.

| Scenario | What it should reveal |
| --- | --- |
| Topic only | A worthwhile angle without unnecessary questions or invented authority. |
| Source packet, no thesis | Supported interpretation and counterevidence, rather than summary or forced contrarianism. |
| Conversation-derived piece | A consequential idea that preserves the speaker's position and separates analysis from personal beliefs. |
| Complex technical explanation | Readable depth, conditions, terminology, and causal reasoning. |
| Supplied personal reflection | Authentic scenes, emotional stakes, humor, and unresolved endings without invented memories or compulsory lessons. |
| Competent but flat revision | Better interest and development without relying on obvious jargon or filler removal. |
| Sensitive correspondence | Responsibility, seriousness, uncertainty, and practical details survive voice choices. |
| Short notice | Proportionate factual writing without a forced hook or argument. |

## Review without condition labels

Give reviewers the assignment, authorized evidence, and drafts under shuffled neutral labels; reveal conditions after judgments are recorded. Where practicable, reviewers should not have generated the drafts or seen the changes. Model reviewers aid diagnosis; simulated personas are not independent human readers.

Apply these gates before judging style:

- **Fidelity:** Facts, intended meaning, protected wording, and explicit requirements survive. Examples remain distinguishable from evidence; new factual claims have support.
- **Speaker:** Identity, experience, quotations, credentials, and personal beliefs are warranted by the assignment.
- **Qualifications:** Material uncertainty, counterevidence, baselines, conditions, and the difference between observation and cause remain intact.

Record failures with the passage and controlling source or requirement. Verify mechanical constraints separately, stating the word-count convention where relevant. Distinguish material errors from ambiguous illustrations and minor imprecision. Accepted drafts must pass these gates; engagement cannot compensate for failure.

Compare originality, progression, interest, rhythm, avoidable questions, and editing burden. Originality can be a useful distinction; it need not be unprecedented or provocative. Judge character and restraint against the occasion, not device counts. Cite passages and explain their effect. Allow ties and mixed results; use no numerical self-scores or voice-match percentages.

## Decide and retest

Separate correct instructions from observed performance. Retain supported improvements for the intended purpose without requiring wins in every register or claiming superiority from ties. A boundary can be correct while a generator fails to follow it; report that limit rather than calling the behavior validated. Address material regressions and keep failed drafts out of accepted output.

After a material defect, make a focused repair and retest on fresh affected material. If the same failure persists, stop phrase-level tightening: change the mechanism or document the limitation. Repeat broader comparisons only when they could change the decision. Known briefs can diagnose repairs, not demonstrate transfer. Batch package checks and repeat only those affected by later edits. Human feedback addresses reader experience; cross-model trials address portability. Neither proves universal reliability.

Keep raw drafts, source packets, conversation excerpts, and private reviews outside the public repository. Publish only authorized sanitized summaries or labeled synthetic examples. Distinguish proposed scenarios, actual outcomes, model review, human review, and cross-model validation when updating the evidence reference.
