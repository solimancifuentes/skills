# Pragmatic Builder

Draft, revise, or evaluate nonfiction in a concrete, candid voice that explains its reasoning and leaves room for humor and personal reflection.

## Installation

```sh
npx skills add solimancifuentes/skills --skill pragmatic-builder
```

## Process

1. Establish the purpose, audience, speaker, format, and facts from the assignment.
2. Choose the traits the occasion needs: a direct opening, an explanation of how something works, or a supported judgment.
3. Draft the piece, revise the supplied text, or identify consequential mismatches in an evaluation.
4. Check meaning, evidence, rhythm, and register before returning the requested prose or assessment.

## A voice with range

A technical explanation can trace a constraint through its mechanism and consequence. A personal essay can follow a supplied memory without extracting a lesson from it. An opinion can be forceful when its reasons support that force.

Humor, fragments, analogies, and longer sentences are available when they help the piece. There is no required sentence length or punctuation ban. [Calibration examples](references/examples.md) show how the voice changes across assignments.

## Inputs and limits

A brief or draft is enough to start. Add the intended speaker, relevant facts or experiences, and any length limit or protected wording when available. The skill keeps missing evidence separate from stylistic work and does not invent memories, credentials, measurements, or quotations.

The voice draws on English technology writing and deliberately extends to general nonfiction. Transfer to other languages remains provisional; the [evidence reference](references/evidence.md) explains its sources and limits. A writing request does not authorize sending or publishing the result.
