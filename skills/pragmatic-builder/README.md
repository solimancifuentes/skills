# Pragmatic Builder

Draft, revise, or evaluate essays, articles, social posts, short posts, comments, and original quotable lines in a concrete, candid voice that explains its reasoning and leaves room for humor and personal reflection. Develop original pieces from a topic, conversation context, task materials, or research; a supplied draft or thesis is optional.

## Installation

```sh
npx skills add solimancifuentes/skills --skill pragmatic-builder
```

## Process

1. Establish the purpose, audience, speaker, format, and facts from the assignment.
2. For substantial original work, find useful material and choose a supported angle. Choose the voice traits the occasion needs.
3. Draft the piece, revise the supplied text, or identify consequential mismatches in an evaluation.
4. Check consequential claims against the supplied account or evidence, repair unsupported assertions, and review progression, rhythm, and register. Keep short tasks proportionate.

## A voice with range

A technical explanation can trace a constraint through its mechanism and consequence. A personal essay can follow a supplied memory without extracting a lesson from it. An opinion can be forceful when its reasons support that force.

Humor, fragments, analogies, and longer sentences are available when they help the piece. There is no required sentence length or punctuation ban. [Calibration examples](references/examples.md) show how the voice changes across assignments.

## Inputs and limits

A topic, brief, draft, conversation context, or source packet is enough to start. Add the intended speaker, relevant facts or experiences, and any length limit or protected wording when available. Original analysis can develop a supported thesis; revision preserves the established stance, and personal writing requires supplied experience. Clearly labeled hypotheticals and analogies can illustrate a point. Editorial freedom does not authorize new factual or personal assertions about the speaker. The instructions prohibit fabrication; they do not guarantee a generator's compliance.

Research is optional and depends on the task and available access. For research assignments, inspect accessible sources, consider consequential counterevidence, and distinguish reported claims from analysis. If access is unavailable, use supplied evidence and disclose material limits. No provider-specific tools or other writing skills are required.

The voice draws on English technology writing and deliberately extends to general nonfiction. Transfer to other languages remains provisional; the [evidence reference](references/evidence.md) explains its sources and limits. A writing request does not authorize sending or publishing the result.

[Evaluation guidance](references/evaluation.md) describes comparative checks for calibration and maintenance. Synthetic examples illustrate editorial choices; they do not establish human engagement or cross-model reliability.
