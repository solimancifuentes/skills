# Pragmatic Builder

A topic is enough to start. Pragmatic Builder can develop original nonfiction from conversation notes, task materials, or research, revise a draft, or evaluate how a piece fits the voice. Its language is concrete and candid, with reasoning the reader can follow and room for humor, reflection, or a forceful opinion.

Use it for essays, articles, posts, comments, and original quotable lines. For substantial original pieces, the aim is to develop an angle that helps readers understand, notice, reconsider, or feel something. A supplied thesis is optional.

## Installation

```sh
npx skills add solimancifuentes/skills --skill pragmatic-builder
```

## Use it

Ask for the voice by name:

> Use Pragmatic Builder to develop a short essay from these conversation notes.

Add the audience, length limit, required stance, or protected wording when they matter. The skill infers ordinary choices from context and asks a narrow question when missing facts, a speaker, or an intended stance would materially change the work.

## How it works

1. Use the assignment’s material and constraints.
2. For substantial original work, consider different angles and choose one the evidence supports.
3. Develop the piece so each passage adds something. Preserve the established stance when revising.
4. Check consequential factual and speaker claims against their sources, then review progression, rhythm, and fit to the occasion.

A short notice needs only the choices that help it.

## A voice with range

A technical explanation can trace a constraint through its mechanism and consequence. A personal essay can follow a supplied memory and leave its meaning unresolved. An opinion can be forceful when its reasons support that force.

Humor, fragments, analogies, digressions, and longer sentences are available when they serve the piece. There is no sentence-length quota or punctuation ban. [Examples](references/examples.md) show the range.

## Evidence and limits

Original analysis can develop supported conclusions. Personal accounts require authentic supplied experience. Do not invent a speaker’s memories, feelings, credentials, or commitments. Hypotheticals and analogies must remain recognizable as illustrations. Attributed quotations need a source.

For research assignments, inspect accessible sources, consider consequential counterevidence, and distinguish reported claims from analysis. When access is unavailable, work within supplied evidence and disclose material limits. No provider-specific tools or other writing skills are required.

The voice draws on English technology writing and extends to general nonfiction. Transfer to other languages remains provisional. The instructions prohibit fabrication; they do not guarantee that a model will comply. Synthetic examples illustrate editorial choices; they do not establish human engagement or cross-model reliability.

[Evidence](references/evidence.md) explains provenance. [Evaluation guidance](references/evaluation.md) supports calibration and maintenance. Neither is required for ordinary drafting. A writing request does not authorize sending or publishing the result.
