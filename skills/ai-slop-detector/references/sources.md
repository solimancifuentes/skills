# Provenance and design boundary

This skill implements a 57-pattern editorial synthesis. Version 2.0 retains the catalog, dimension assignments, weights, arithmetic, and operating thresholds of version 1.0, while adding explicit evidence scope, consequence/extent records, and illustrative anchors. Public-release reporting is concise by default, with a full profile when requested; report detail does not change the assessment schema or scoring. These remain heuristic design choices. The upstream projects and research below do not validate this specific instrument. No pretrained classifier, human calibration dataset, or authorship model is included.

Primary influences:

- Peter Yang, [no-ai-slop](https://github.com/petergyang/no-ai-slop/tree/000650b156983f5159695b441477f4e63b25dc85): [editing rules](https://github.com/petergyang/no-ai-slop/blob/000650b156983f5159695b441477f4e63b25dc85/skills/no-ai-slop/SKILL.md), [evaluation checklist](https://github.com/petergyang/no-ai-slop/blob/000650b156983f5159695b441477f4e63b25dc85/skills/no-ai-slop/eval.md), and supporting plugin material. Main contributions: preserve voice/meaning, minimum effective edits, quote observable patterns, and do not guess AI authorship.
- Hardik Pandya, [stop-slop](https://github.com/hardikpandya/stop-slop): [core](https://github.com/hardikpandya/stop-slop/blob/8da1f030185bdfe8471220585162991eaeb970e9/SKILL.md), [phrases](https://github.com/hardikpandya/stop-slop/blob/8da1f030185bdfe8471220585162991eaeb970e9/references/phrases.md), [structures](https://github.com/hardikpandya/stop-slop/blob/8da1f030185bdfe8471220585162991eaeb970e9/references/structures.md), and [examples](https://github.com/hardikpandya/stop-slop/blob/8da1f030185bdfe8471220585162991eaeb970e9/references/examples.md). Main contributions: rhetorical, rhythmic, and filler categories. Its blanket grammar bans and unanchored 35/50 cutoff are not adopted. Links now identify revision `8da1f030185bdfe8471220585162991eaeb970e9`, whose core and MIT notice were checked for this release. Original development reads used the moving main branch, so the historical reads are not claimed to be an exact snapshot of this revision.
- Lauren Tan / Cursor, [pstack](https://github.com/cursor/plugins/tree/fadd23794c0075468eb8964b0fd93e06e09486ad/pstack): [unslop](https://github.com/cursor/plugins/blob/fadd23794c0075468eb8964b0fd93e06e09486ad/pstack/skills/unslop/SKILL.md), [technical-writing](https://github.com/cursor/plugins/blob/fadd23794c0075468eb8964b0fd93e06e09486ad/pstack/skills/technical-writing/SKILL.md), [teach](https://github.com/cursor/plugins/blob/fadd23794c0075468eb8964b0fd93e06e09486ad/pstack/skills/teach/SKILL.md), and [epistemics](https://github.com/cursor/plugins/blob/fadd23794c0075468eb8964b0fd93e06e09486ad/pstack/skills/why/references/epistemics.md). Main contributions: reader cost, over-compression, contextual exceptions, calibrated uncertainty, and source fidelity.

These links identify design influences, not a reproducible evaluation dataset or evidence of detector accuracy. Historical repository-search counts are omitted because the corresponding review records are not distributed with this skill.

The inherited ideas are contextualized rather than treated as author-identification signals. Automatic punctuation bans, all-adverb bans, universal human-subject requirements, and fixed list-length preferences are excluded. Source prose is not imported as active skill instructions. Attribution and upstream permission notices are retained in [THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.txt](../THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.txt).

## Measurement research informing version 2.0

Reviewed 2026-09-25. These links document design reasoning, not live runtime dependencies. This targeted review was not a systematic review. Conclusions below are limited to the cited tasks; the protocol in validation.md is our proposed application.

| Source | Relevant finding or guidance | Limit for this skill |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [Shaib et al., Measuring AI “Slop” in Text, arXiv v2](https://arxiv.org/html/2509.19163v2) | Expert slop judgments and automated detection remain difficult in English news/QA. | Does not validate this rubric or its use with other evaluators. Span table/prose discrepancies make exact extraction figures unsuitable as design constants. |
| [AERA/APA/NCME, Standards, 2014](https://www.testingstandards.net/uploads/7/6/6/4/76643089/standards_2014edition.pdf) | Validate intended interpretations, including profiles; use scoring examples and check report comprehension. | Professional testing guidance transferred by analogy, not slop certification. |
| [Liu et al., ECBD, ACL 2024](https://aclanthology.org/2024.acl-long.861/) | Justify evidence extraction and accumulation separately. | A benchmark-design framework, not an optimal formula. |
| [Xiao et al., MetricEval, EMNLP 2023](https://aclanthology.org/2023.emnlp-main.676/) | Separate evaluation stability, sample sensitivity, and construct validity. | Summarization case study; no universal reliability threshold. |
| [OECD/JRC, composite-indicator handbook, 2008](https://www.oecd.org/content/dam/oecd/en/publications/reports/2008/08/handbook-on-constructing-composite-indicators-methodology-and-user-guide_g1gh9301/9789264043466-en.pdf) | Weights encode priorities; aggregation allows tradeoffs; test sensitivity. | General composite methodology, not empirically calibrated editorial penalties. |
| [MQM scoring](https://www.themqm.org/mqm-pillars/the-mqm-scoring-models/) and [repeated errors](https://www.themqm.org/guidance/repeatederrors/) | Distinguish consequence, error identity, and repeated exposure. | Configurable translation conventions; numeric penalties are not imported. |
| [Freitag et al., TACL 2021](https://aclanthology.org/2021.tacl-1.87/) | Explicit contextual error annotation improved particular translation evaluations. | Does not establish expert superiority for every reader outcome. |
| [Norman, 2010](https://doi.org/10.1007/s10459-010-9222-y) and [Liddell & Kruschke, 2018](https://scholarworks.iu.edu/dspace/items/b676ceeb-4bd8-45cb-b252-6ff9fda86c34) | Ordinal numerical summaries and latent equal-interval interpretation are different issues; robustness depends on conditions. | Neither validates these categories as measured units. Liddell/Kruschke reviewed through institutional abstract/publisher preview. |
| [Howcroft & Rieser, EMNLP 2021](https://aclanthology.org/2021.emnlp-main.703/) | Ordinal models improved power in tested NLG simulations. | Model assumptions and adequate data still matter. |
| [Hallgren, 2012](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC3402032/) | Match agreement statistics to rater design and the reported quantity. | Reliability alone does not establish validity. |
| [Bollen & Lennox, 1991](https://doi.org/10.1037/0033-2909.110.2.305) | Internal-consistency assumptions depend on how components relate to the construct. | Viewing editorial costs as a designed composite is our interpretation. |
| [Tang et al., NAACL 2022](https://aclanthology.org/2022.naacl-main.417/) and [Jeong et al., BlackboxNLP 2025](https://aclanthology.org/2025.blackboxnlp-1.5/) | Relative and absolute elicitation methods differ by task and data. | No universal format winner; latter is workshop evidence. |
| [Panickssery et al., NeurIPS 2024](https://proceedings.neurips.cc/paper_files/paper/2024/file/7f1f0218e45f5414c79c0679633e47bc-Paper-Conference.pdf) | Self-recognition/preference occurred in studied summarization judges. | Not proof of bias in this particular assessment. |
| [Card et al., EMNLP 2020](https://aclanthology.org/2020.emnlp-main.745/) | Power depends on item/rater variation and effect size. | No universal prompt or rater count follows. |
| [Belz & Thomson, HEDS 3.0, GEM 2025](https://aclanthology.org/2025.gem-1.6/) | Document sampling, raters, instructions, and analysis. | Reporting completeness is not empirical validity. |

The anchors are original synthetic illustrations authored during development. They are not independent human labels or a validation dataset. A release that passes behavioral tests remains unvalidated for model-population rankings and reader-burden claims until the corresponding empirical work is performed.
