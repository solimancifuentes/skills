# Company Report Template

Default structure for the inline report. Adapt to category, evidence, and depth mode: drop sections that don't apply, never pad. Section order below is the default order. Quick mode does not require a dedicated timeline and omits FAQs unless requested; all other modes keep FAQs optional and nonduplicative.

---

## Company Intelligence Profile: [Company Name]

### Snapshot

Compact table or tight field list. Include ONLY fields with real values; move missing ones to Sidenotes.

Candidate fields (pick what applies):
- Company (legal/common name)
- Website
- Category (from classification)
- Status (operating / acquired / IPO'd / shut down / stealth)
- Founded (year — HQ city)
- Headquarters
- Ticker & exchange or OTC market (target's confirmed publicly traded equity only)
- Ownership (public / VC-backed / PE-owned / family-owned / bootstrapped / parent company)
- CEO
- Founders
- Employees (with source + "est." label if estimated)
- Funding (total; latest round + date)
- Valuation (source + date; private only, source-reported only)
- Revenue / ARR / MRR (only if disclosed or credibly reported; with period)
- Market cap & stock price (target's confirmed publicly traded equity only; with date)
- Reporting date (requested as-of date, or current date)
- Researched on (retrieval date, when different from reporting date)

### Executive Summary
1–3 short paragraphs: what the company does, current stage/status, and the most important business/financial context.

### Company Journey & Milestones  *(companies without publicly traded equity; when useful)*
Up to 10 supported, dated one-line bullets, with no minimum:
founding context → early product/launch → funding series or bootstrap milestones → major launches, pivots, partnerships, expansions, acquisitions → current stage. Omit the section when evidence is sparse or it does not serve the requested depth. For publicly traded companies, fold history into the Overview instead.

### Company Overview
Product, market, customers, and business model in clear plain language.

### Status & Classification
Category + one or two lines of reasoning based on the exact entity and reporting date. Distinguish reporting obligations, offering progress, and equity trading when they differ; filings or historical/proposed tickers alone do not establish current public stock availability.

### Financial & Funding Profile
Category-adapted. Only source-supported fields. Candidate fields: total funding, rounds, latest round, investors, valuation, revenue, ARR/MRR, profitability, market cap, stock price, IPO price, cash/debt, key public financials, acquisition amount. Every figure gets a date/period.

### Leadership & Key People
CEO, founders, and major executives (CFO/COO/CTO/CPO) when relevant; board/notable investors when relevant. Brief background only when it adds signal.

### Products, Services & Business Model
Main products/services, revenue model, target customers, positioning, pricing model if public.

### Market, Competitors & Positioning
Main competitors, differentiation, market category, key advantages, key weaknesses/challenges.

### Recent Signals & Insights
Material signals only (not a news dump): recent news, launches, hiring trends, partnerships, funding activity, customer wins, regulatory/legal issues, market movement if public. Distinguish reported facts, source estimates, and analytical inferences. In sales mode, label possible needs or outreach angles as hypotheses rather than facts about the company.

### Risks, Open Questions & Watch Items
Business, financial, competitive, and execution risks; data gaps; what to monitor next.

### FAQs  *(optional)*
Include only useful questions not already answered in the report, without a minimum count. Omit in quick mode unless requested. Possible topics, when relevant and not duplicative: ownership, founders, business model, funding, valuation, competitors, profitability, or stock availability.

### Sources
Most important sources, authoritative first. Use inline citations if the environment supports them.

### Sidenotes / Unavailable Information
One line per expected-but-missing field. Unsuccessful searches establish only what was not found in the reviewed sources. Reserve nondisclosure claims for supporting evidence and attribute them. Examples:
- ARR/MRR not found in the sources reviewed.
- Current private valuation not found in the sources reviewed.
- Company states it does not publicly disclose revenue (cite the statement and date, when available).
- Employee count is an estimate and varies by source.

## Category-specific emphasis

**Public company** — Overview; ticker/exchange or OTC market/market cap/price + recent movement when public equity trading is confirmed; IPO date if relevant and confirmed; revenue, profit/loss, margins, cash/debt; business segments; executives & board; latest annual/quarterly filing highlights; recent news; risks & opportunities; optional FAQs; sources. Qualify reporting status separately when it does not establish public equity trading.

**Recently IPO'd** — Confirmed completed IPO date, IPO price & exchange; current price vs. IPO price when trading is confirmed; market cap; S-1/prospectus highlights; revenue growth, losses, margins, cash; lockup/post-IPO considerations if public & relevant; major shareholders/founders/executives; filing-disclosed risks; recent market performance; optional FAQs; sources.

**Private startup (incl. solo/indie/scaleup)** — Overview; stage if inferable; founded; HQ; founders; CEO/executives; product summary; business model; funding rounds & total; last known valuation (source-reported only); investors; employee estimate; traction signals (customers, partnerships, usage, traffic, hiring, launches); ARR/MRR/revenue only if disclosed/credibly reported; supported Journey & Milestones when useful; competitive landscape; recent news; optional FAQs; sources.

**Private non-startup** — Overview; ownership type; founded; HQ; executives; business model; revenue estimate only if source-reported; employee estimate; locations/markets; major products; parent/subsidiaries if applicable; supported Journey & Milestones when useful; recent news; optional FAQs; sources.

**Acquired / subsidiary** — Overview; current parent; acquisition date & amount if disclosed; acquirer rationale; current operating status; founders/executives where relevant; product summary; integration/brand status; recent news; optional FAQs; sources. Do not substitute parent stock or financial figures for the target's own; identify separately reported segment information explicitly.

**Nonprofit / foundation** — Mission; founding date; HQ; leadership; funding model; grants/donations/assets if available; programs; public filings (e.g., Form 990 or equivalent) if available; impact indicators; recent news; optional FAQs; sources.

**Government-owned / state-linked** — Overview; owning entity & governance; mandate; funding/budget if public; leadership; scope of operations; recent news; sources.

**Stealth / limited info** — Known facts only: website/domain; founders/executives if publicly listed; funding if disclosed; hiring signals; product/category hints; explicit "what is unknown"; sources. Keep it short and honest.

**Unknown / insufficient data** — State what was searched, what little was found, and ask the user for a disambiguating detail (domain, location, industry).
